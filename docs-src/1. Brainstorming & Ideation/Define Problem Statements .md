## Define Problem Statements (Customer Problem Statement Template)

FitBuddy helps people who want a structured fitness routine but do not know how to convert their personal goals and preferences into a practical weekly workout plan.

| Problem Statement (PS) | I am (Customer) | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS-1 | A beginner starting a fitness routine | Build a weekly workout plan that matches my goal | Generic plans may not suit my age, weight or preferred intensity | Advice is often broad and not personalized | Uncertain about where to start |
| PS-2 | A user working toward weight loss or muscle gain | Follow a structured seven-day schedule | I do not know how to organize exercises across the week | Exercise choices and intensity are difficult to plan manually | Overwhelmed by options |
| PS-3 | A user whose needs change after seeing a plan | Modify the routine | Rebuilding the entire plan manually takes time | Feedback is naturally expressed in plain language | Frustrated by repeated planning |
| PS-4 | A student or busy professional | Get a plan quickly and revisit it later | I lose track of earlier plans | There is no single saved workspace | Relieved when the plan is persistent |
